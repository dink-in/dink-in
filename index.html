About Our Club

Welcome to Dink-In Pickleball Club

Dink-In is more than just a pickleball club – it's a community dedicated to bringing people together through the fastest-growing sport in America. Whether you're a seasoned player or picking up a paddle for the first time, you'll find your place here.

Our mission is to promote a healthy, active lifestyle while fostering friendships and friendly competition. We host regular tournaments, skill-building sessions, and social events for players of all levels.

  • Professional coaching available
  • Courts available 7 days a week
  • Beginner-friendly community
  • Monthly tournaments and leagues
